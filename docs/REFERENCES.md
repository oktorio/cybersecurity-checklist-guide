## Reference-precision rule

The Excel working paper has a **Reference Precision** field:
- `Validated exact timeline` / `Validated exact statutory clause`: the specific clause or deadline was directly validated for v3.1.
- `Section-level / control mapping`: useful supervisory mapping, but the exact operative clause must be rechecked before a formal breach citation.
- `Guidance / benchmark`: non-statutory or international guidance used to strengthen control design/testing.

## Maintenance rule

Before each formal review, check OJK/BI/JDIH sources for new regulations, amendments, FAQs, reporting instructions, transition rules, or supervisory directions that may change scope, deadlines, submission channels, or legal criteria.
